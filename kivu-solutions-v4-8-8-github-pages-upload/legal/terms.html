Kivu Solutions Terms of Service

Launch draft — 28 September 2026. Review with qualified counsel before broad rollout.

These terms govern business use of Kivu Solutions, a service operated by Platinum Tech.

Accounts and authorized users

The business owner is responsible for account information, authorized staff, device access and actions taken through authorized accounts. Credentials and staff PINs must not be shared outside the people authorized by the business.

Plans and billing

Features vary by plan. A plan request does not activate a paid plan until Platinum Tech confirms payment and activation. Trial, grace, suspension and cancellation rules shown in the application form part of the service configuration.

Business records

The business is responsible for the accuracy of products, prices, tax settings, customer data, opening stock and other records entered into Kivu. The business should maintain appropriate independent records and periodically export/back up critical information.

Offline sales

Offline retail sales are queued drafts until synchronized. The server may reject a queued sale if stock, permissions, subscription status or related data no longer permit it. A final Kivu receipt is issued after successful synchronization.

Taxes and EBM

Kivu's tax/VAT fields and normal sales receipts do not by themselves make Kivu an RRA Electronic Billing Machine or Electronic Invoicing System. A business subject to RRA fiscal-invoicing requirements remains responsible for using an approved EBM/EIS solution unless and until Platinum Tech provides a separately approved integration.

Payments

A payment method label such as “Mobile Money” records how a sale was paid. Unless a specific integrated payment feature is expressly enabled, Kivu does not itself collect, hold or settle the customer's money.

Acceptable use

Users must not bypass access controls, interfere with the service, use another business's data, upload unlawful content, or use Kivu to falsify sales, tax, stock or financial records.

Service availability

Platinum Tech aims to provide a reliable service but does not guarantee uninterrupted availability. Connectivity, third-party hosting, device failure and external service providers can affect availability. Offline mode is intended to reduce—not eliminate—this risk.

Suspension

Access may be suspended for non-payment, security risk, misuse, legal requirements or material breach. Where appropriate, Platinum Tech should provide notice and a route to resolve the issue.

Liability and support

Commercial liability limits, support commitments, refund rules and governing-law/dispute clauses should be finalized by Platinum Tech with qualified counsel before broad commercial rollout.

Important: This document intentionally avoids pretending unfinished integrations are live. EBM certification, automated Mobile Money collection and full multi-branch management require separate production implementation/approval.
