Kivu Solutions Privacy Notice

Launch draft — 28 September 2026. Review with qualified counsel before broad rollout.

Kivu Solutions is operated by Platinum Tech. This notice explains how personal data may be processed when a business and its authorized staff use Kivu Solutions.

Data we process

Depending on the features used, this may include account identity and contact details, staff roles, customer and supplier details entered by the business, sales and receipt information, device/session identifiers, audit logs, support communications and technical security records.

Why we process it

We process information to provide the service, authenticate users, operate business records, prevent misuse, provide support, maintain audit and security controls, comply with legal obligations and improve reliability.

Business customer responsibilities

A business using Kivu Solutions is responsible for ensuring it has an appropriate legal basis to enter and use personal data relating to its customers, staff and suppliers and for giving any notices required by law.

Access and security

Kivu uses tenant separation, role-based permissions, row-level security, controlled database functions, audit logging and device-session controls. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Business owners should protect credentials, review staff access and promptly disable accounts that should no longer have access.

Offline storage

When offline mode is used, an authorized device may temporarily retain a recent business snapshot and queued retail sales locally so work can continue. Users should secure the device with a screen lock and remove access from lost or shared devices.

Retention and deletion

Business records are retained for the period needed to provide the service and meet applicable contractual, accounting, tax, security and legal requirements. Some audit or transaction records may need to be preserved even after an account is closed.

Your rights

Rwanda's Law Nº 058/2021 of 13/10/2021 governs protection of personal data and privacy. Requests concerning access, correction, objection, restriction or other applicable rights should be submitted through the Kivu Solutions support channel shown in the application. Platinum Tech will verify the requester and respond in line with applicable law.

Processors and hosting

Kivu Solutions relies on technology service providers, including its database/authentication hosting provider. Platinum Tech should maintain appropriate processor contracts and document any cross-border processing before broad rollout.

Incidents

Suspected unauthorized access or data loss should be reported promptly through the Kivu Solutions support channel so the account can be secured and applicable notification duties assessed.

Important: This is a product launch draft, not a legal opinion. Platinum Tech should complete its controller/processor assessment, retention schedule, processor list, contact details and any registration/compliance steps required by Rwanda's Data Protection & Privacy Office.

Official reference: Rwanda Data Protection & Privacy Office, Law Nº 058/2021 of 13/10/2021 — dpo.gov.rw.
