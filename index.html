FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common billing questions

Can I switch or upgrade my plan anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade from Lite to Business or Control whenever your business hires staff or needs advanced auditing. Upgrades take effect immediately upon payment confirmation.

What happens to my data if my subscription lapses?

Your business data is 100% safe and permanently preserved in the database. Operational access pauses temporarily, and instantly resumes once renewed.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Every newly registered business can explore the full features of Kivu Control for 7 days without paying. You only choose a paid plan after experiencing the value.

Are there hidden fees or setup charges?

None. The price you see is the exact amount you pay. Standard software updates, cloud syncing, and offline PWA functionality are all included.

Activation note: Plan change requests are processed by Platinum Tech upon confirmation of Mobile Money or Bank payment. The browser client cannot activate its own paid plan.

7-day free trial: New businesses can test Kivu Control before choosing a paid plan. EBM, payment collection, and multi-branch are not advertised as live until the relevant integration/certification is completed.

Database Procedure Notice

Notice: function gen_random_bytes(integer) does not exist

In Supabase, cryptographic functions live in the extensions schema. Your Postgres procedure create_staff_invite needs extensions added to its search path to run. 

CREATE EXTENSION IF NOT EXISTS pgcrypto WITH SCHEMA extensions;
ALTER FUNCTION public.create_staff_invite(text, text, text) SET search_path = public, extensions;

Paste and run this snippet in your Supabase SQL Editor to enable database staff invites. In the meantime, you can test and preview any employee role right now using the Role Simulator!
