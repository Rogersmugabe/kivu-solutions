# Troubleshooting and questions

## Signing in

**I forgot my password (owner).** On the sign-in screen enter your email and choose **Forgot password?**. Follow the link sent to your email.

**I forgot my PIN (staff).** Ask the owner. There is no PIN reset inside the app yet; the owner contacts Kivu support to reset it.

**"This staff account has been disabled".** An owner or manager disabled your staff account. Ask them to reactivate it.

**The invite code is "not valid".** Check the code and that you entered the same phone number the invite was made for. Codes work once and expire after 14 days — ask for a new one.

**I did not get the confirmation email (owner sign-up).** Check spam, wait a few minutes, then sign up again with the same email.

## Selling

**"Not enough stock".** Kivu will not sell more than it has recorded. Receive the delivery first in **Stock movements**, or correct the count with an adjustment.

**A sale says "waiting to sync".** It was made offline. Keep the app open with internet and it sends itself. If one *failed*, the notice explains why (usually not enough stock); fix the stock and it is retried.

**Can I sell on two phones at once?** Yes. Each person signs in on their own device. Parked sales stay on the device where they were parked.

**The scanner does nothing.** Make sure the barcode is saved on the product (**Products → Edit → Barcode**). USB scanners need the cursor in the search box.

## Printing

**The receipt is cut short or tiny.** In the print window choose the receipt printer, paper 80 mm (or *Roll paper*), margins *None*, scale *100%*, and turn off headers and footers.

**Nothing prints from my phone.** The phone must see the printer — a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi thermal printer with its print service (Android) or AirPrint (iPhone).

## "Not allowed" messages

Kivu checks every action twice: on the screen and in the database. If you see *not allowed* or *permission denied*, your role or plan does not include that action. Ask the owner, or (owners) check **Plan & billing**.

## Install Kivu like an app

- **Android (Chrome):** menu ⋮ → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome / Edge):** the install icon in the address bar.

## Your data

- Money is stored in whole Rwandan Francs.
- Business days follow Kigali time, whatever the device clock says.
- Nothing is deleted when you void or adjust: corrections are new records, so the history stays complete.
- Kivu receipts are not RRA EBM fiscal receipts. If your business must issue EBM receipts, keep using your certified EBM device.
