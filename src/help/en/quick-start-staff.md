# Joining and signing in (staff)

## Join your team (first time)

You need an **invite code** from the owner. It usually arrives on WhatsApp.

1. Open Kivu POS and choose **Create account → Staff**.
2. Enter the **invite code**, your **full name** and the **phone number the invite was made for**.
3. Choose a **6-digit PIN** you will remember. Do not share it.
4. Press **Join team**. You are signed in straight away.

The code works once and expires after 14 days. If it says the code is not valid for your phone number, check both with the owner.

## Sign in

Choose **Sign in → Staff**, enter your phone number and your PIN.

## What you will see

Kivu shows only the pages your role uses:

| Role | Pages |
|---|---|
| Manager | Everything except billing and settings |
| Cashier | Point of sale, Sales, Cash shifts (and Tables in bars and restaurants) |
| Stock clerk | Products and Stock movements |
| Waiter | Tables & orders |
| Kitchen / bar prep | The order queue on Tables & orders |

## Stock clerk

- **Receive a delivery** — **Stock movements → Receive stock**, choose the product (or scan it) and the quantity. You can count in bulk units (e.g. crates).
- **Something missing or damaged?** **Stock movements → Request adjustment**, choose the reason. A manager approves it before stock changes.

## Waiter

- Tap a free table to open a bill, add items with notes (e.g. *no ice*), then press **Send to kitchen / bar**.
- A cashier or manager settles the bill when the guests pay.

## Kitchen / bar prep

- Your screen shows orders sent and not yet served. Prepare them and press **Served**.

## Always

Sign out when you finish (tap your name → **Sign out**). If your account is disabled, ask the owner or a manager.
