# Selling at the counter

Open **Point of sale** from the menu. If you have no open cash shift, Kivu reminds you to open one first so your sales count toward your drawer.

## Add products to the sale

- **Tap** a product card to add one. Tap again to add another.
- **Search** by name or barcode (press **F2** to jump to the search box), or **scan** a barcode:
  - a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner types into the search box and adds the product automatically;
  - on phones and tablets that support it, the **Scan** button uses the camera.
- **Categories** above the products filter the list.
- In the cart, use **+ / −** or type the quantity. The bin icon removes a line.
- **Sell by the crate (or carton):** if a product has a bulk unit, tap *sell by crate* under its name in the cart. The price becomes price × units per crate.

Kivu will not let you sell more than you have in stock. Cards show how many are left; *Out of stock* products cannot be added.

## Take payment

| Method | When |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | Enter **Cash received**; Kivu shows the **change**. The quick buttons fill the exact amount or the next round note. |
| **MoMo** | MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money |
| **Bank** | Card or bank transfer |
| **Split** | Part cash, part MoMo or bank. Enter each part. **Charge** works only when the parts add up exactly to the total. Change is worked out on the cash part. |

**Discount** — owners and managers can enter a discount before charging. Cashiers cannot give discounts.

Press **Charge** (or **Ctrl + Enter**). On a phone, tap **Review items** at the bottom to jump to the cart. The receipt appears: **Print receipt**, then **New sale**.

## Park a sale

A customer goes to fetch money? Press **Park**, add a note (e.g. the customer's name), and serve the next person. Open **Parked** to resume or discard it later.

Parked sales are kept **on this device only**. Finish them on the same phone or computer.

## Selling without internet

When the connection drops, a yellow **Offline** notice appears. Keep selling:

- each sale is saved on the device with a unique reference;
- stock shown on the cards goes down as you sell;
- when the internet returns, sales are sent automatically and you see *"offline sales synced"*.

A sale is never recorded twice, even if it is sent again. If the server refuses a queued sale (for example not enough stock), the notice tells you why.

## Printing receipts

Kivu prints 80 mm (72 mm printable) receipts on thermal printers connected to the phone or computer. In the print window choose your receipt printer, set margins to *None* and scale to *100%*. Long receipts print in full.

> Kivu receipts are not RRA EBM fiscal receipts.
