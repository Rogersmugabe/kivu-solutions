# Tables & orders (bars and restaurants)

This page appears when the business type is **Bar** or **Restaurant** (**Settings → Business type**) on Kivu Business and higher.

## Floor

- **Add table** (owners and managers) — a name and number of seats.
- Tap a free table and **Open bill**, or choose *No table (counter / takeaway)*.
- The cards show tables in use, open bills and their total value.

## Take an order

1. Open the bill, choose the **item**, **quantity** and a **note** (e.g. *no ice*, *well done*), then add it.
2. Press **Send to kitchen / bar**. Items show *Not sent*, *In kitchen* or *Served*.

### Complimentary (free) items

Tick *complimentary* when adding the item. Owners and managers add it straight away. Others give a reason and the request goes to **Approvals** (Kivu Control).

## Kitchen & bar queue

Kitchen and bar staff see orders sent and not yet served, with notes. Prepare each one and press **Served**.

## Settle a bill

Owners, managers and cashiers can settle.

1. Open the bill and press **Settle**.
2. Choose how the guests paid. **Add payment method** to split the bill between cash, MoMo and bank — the parts must add up to the bill total.
3. **Close bill**. It is recorded as a sale and the stock goes down. Cash paid counts toward the open cash shift.

## Recipes (owners and managers)

A recipe lists the ingredients used each time a menu item is sold — for example a cocktail uses 0.05 L of gin and 1 tonic. When the item is sold, the ingredients' stock goes down automatically.

**Recipes** → choose the menu item → **Add ingredient** with the quantity per item → **Save recipe**.

## Wastage (owners and managers)

Spills, breakages or spoiled food: **Wastage** → item, quantity, reason → **Record wastage**. Stock is reduced and the loss appears in **Stock movements**.
