# Cash shifts, expenses and suppliers

## Cash shifts (Kivu Business and higher)

A shift ties the cash in a drawer to one person, from opening to closing.

### Open a shift

**Cash shifts → Open shift**, enter the **opening float** (cash already in the drawer). Each person runs one shift at a time.

### During the shift

- **Print X-report** prints the shift so far, without closing it.
- **Pay out cash** (owners and managers) — money taken from the drawer for a small purchase. It is saved as a *Petty cash* expense linked to the shift, so it comes off the expected cash.

### Close a shift

1. Count **all** the cash in the drawer.
2. **Close shift**, enter the **cash counted**.
3. A Z-report is available to print from the shift history.

**Expected cash** = opening float + cash sales (including the cash part of split payments and cash paid for table bills) − voided cash − cash paid out.

- **Cashiers do a blind count**: they do not see the expected amount, so the count is honest. If the count does not match, they must write a note to close.
- **Owners and managers** see the expected amount and whether they are short or over, and must give a reason for any difference.

Differences appear in **Shift history** (*Balanced*, or the amount short / over with the reason) and under **Approvals → Exceptions** on Kivu Control.

## Expenses

**Expenses** records money spent by the business — rent, salaries, utilities, transport, supplies, repairs, taxes & fees, marketing, petty cash and other. Only owners and managers can record expenses.

- **Add expense**: description, amount, category.
- Filter by period and category; the chart shows where money goes.
- **Export CSV** for your accountant.

Expenses reduce **net profit** in Reports. A *Petty cash* expense recorded during your open shift is taken out of your expected drawer cash.

## Suppliers and purchases (Kivu Business and higher)

**Suppliers** keeps track of who you buy from and how much you owe.

1. **Add supplier** — name, phone, address, notes.
2. **Record purchase** — choose the supplier and add a line per product: quantity, unit (base or bulk), cost per unit and expiry. Enter the invoice number, the **amount paid now**, how it was paid and when the rest is due. The stock is added straight away and the cost price updated.
3. **Record payment** when you pay off what you owe.

The cards show the total owed to suppliers, how many suppliers you owe and purchases over 30 days. Open a supplier to see their purchases and payments.
