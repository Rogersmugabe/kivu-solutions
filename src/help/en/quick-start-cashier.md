# Cashier quick start

## Every shift

1. **Sign in** — choose *Sign in → Staff*, enter your phone number and your 6-digit PIN.
2. **Open your shift** — go to **Cash shifts → Open shift** and enter the cash already in the drawer (the float).
3. **Sell** — go to **Point of sale** (or press **F2**).
4. **Close your shift** — count all the cash in the drawer, go to **Cash shifts → Close shift** and enter the total. A Z-report prints.
5. **Sign out** — tap your name at the top right → **Sign out**, so nobody sells under your name.

## Making a sale

1. Tap the products, or scan their barcodes. Tap again to add more, or use **+ / −**.
2. Choose the payment: **Cash**, **MoMo**, **Bank** or **Split** (part cash, part MoMo or bank).
3. For cash, enter what the customer gave you — Kivu shows the **change**.
4. Press **Charge**. Print the receipt if the customer wants one, then press **New sale**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Wrong item sold?** Go to **Sales**, open the receipt and press **Request void**. A manager approves it.
- **Customer goes to get money?** Press **Park**, serve the next customer, then open **Parked** to finish the sale.
- **No internet?** Keep selling. Sales are saved on the device and sent automatically when the internet returns.
- **Forgot your PIN?** Ask the owner to reset your staff account.

Cashiers cannot give discounts. Ask a manager or the owner.
