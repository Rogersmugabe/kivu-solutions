# Staff, approvals and reports

## Staff & roles (Kivu Business and higher)

Every person signs in with their own account, so every sale, void and stock change shows who did it.

### Invite someone (owners)

1. **Staff & roles → Invite staff**.
2. Enter their **full name**, **phone number** and **role**.
3. **Create invite**. Send the code with **Send on WhatsApp**, **Share** or **Copy message** — the message explains how to join.

The code works once, only for that phone number, and expires after 14 days. Unused codes are listed under **Pending invites**.

### Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, settings and staff invites |
| Manager | Runs the shop: selling, stock, suppliers, expenses, approvals and reports; can disable staff. No invites, billing or settings |
| Cashier | Sells, runs their own cash shift and sees their own sales. No discounts |
| Stock clerk | Receives stock and manages products. Cannot sell or see money totals |
| Waiter | Opens table bills and sends orders to the kitchen |
| Kitchen / bar prep | Sees the order queue and marks orders as served |

These limits are enforced by the database as well as the screens: hiding a button is never the only protection.

### Disable or reactivate

Owners and managers can do this. When someone leaves, press **Disable** on their row. They can no longer sign in, but their history stays. **Reactivate** restores access.

## Approvals & activity (Kivu Control and higher)

**Waiting for approval** lists requests from staff:

- **Void a sale** — from a cashier;
- **Stock adjustment** — from a stock clerk;
- **Complimentary item** — a free item on a table bill.

Read the reason, then **Approve** or **Reject**. Nothing changes until it is approved.

**Exceptions** shows voids and cash differences at shift close. The **Audit log** lists the latest actions by your team — who did what, and when.

## Dashboard

The first page owners and managers see: today's sales, receipts, gross profit and expenses, the last 7 days, how customers paid, best sellers, recent receipts and **stock alerts** (low and out of stock, plus stock value at cost).

## Reports

Choose a **period** to see:

- **Sales**, **gross profit** (sales − cost of goods), **expenses** and **net profit**;
- sales over time and how customers paid;
- the best-selling products and sales by staff member.

**Export for accountant** downloads the figures as a spreadsheet file.

Profit is only as accurate as your **cost prices** — keep them up to date when you receive stock.
