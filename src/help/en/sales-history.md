# Sales history, receipts and voids

Go to **Sales** to see every receipt: when, who served, how it was paid and the total.

- **Filter** by period (today, 7, 30 or 90 days), payment method and status, or **search** by receipt number, product or staff name.
- The cards at the top show takings for the period, split into **Cash**, **Mobile Money** and **Bank** (split payments and table bills are divided by how they were really paid).
- **View** opens a receipt with each line and how it was paid. **Print receipt** reprints it.
- **Export CSV** downloads the sales for a spreadsheet or your accountant.

Cashiers see their own sales; owners and managers see everyone's.

## Voiding a sale (Kivu Control and higher)

Voiding cancels a line, puts the stock back and removes the money from the totals. Open the receipt and, on the line to cancel:

- **Owners and managers** press **Void** and give a reason. It happens immediately.
- **Cashiers** press **Request void** and give a reason. The request goes to **Approvals**; stock and totals change only when a manager approves it.

Voided lines stay visible, marked *Voided*, with the reason — nothing is deleted. On shift close, the cash expected for a voided line is removed (for split payments, in proportion).
