# Getting started (owners)

Kivu POS helps you sell, track every item of stock and control your cash. This guide takes you from a new account to your first sale in about 15 minutes.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open Kivu POS and choose **Create account → Owner**.
2. Enter your name, your business name, your email and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. Open the confirmation email we send you and click the link, then sign in.

New businesses start with a **7-day free trial of Kivu Control**, so you can try every feature.

## 2. Set up your business

Go to **Settings**:

- **Business** — name, phone number and **business type**. Choose *Bar*, *Restaurant* or *Bar & restaurant* to switch on **Tables & orders**.
- **Receipt footer** — a short message printed at the bottom of every receipt, for example *Murakoze!*
- **Tax** — if your prices include VAT, enter the rate (for example 18) and your TIN. Receipts then show the VAT included in the total.
- **Location** — province, district, sector and cell.

> Kivu receipts are not RRA EBM fiscal receipts. If your business is VAT-registered, keep issuing EBM receipts as RRA requires.

## 3. Add your products

Go to **Products → Add product**. For each product enter:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Name and category | How you want it to appear at the till, e.g. *Primus 72cl*, *Drinks* |
| Barcode | Scan it with the camera button or a barcode scanner, or type it |
| Selling price | The price of **one** base unit |
| Cost price | What one base unit costs you. Used for profit and stock value |
| Base unit / bulk unit | How you sell (Bottle) and how you buy (Crate) |
| Base units per bulk unit | e.g. 12 bottles per crate |
| Low-stock alert | Kivu warns you when stock falls to this level |
| Opening stock | How many you have today |

**Many products?** Use **Import CSV**. The first line must name the columns, for example: `name,price,cost,qty,category,barcode,low_stock`.

After a product is created, its quantity only changes through sales and **Stock movements**, so every change is recorded.

## 4. Invite your staff (Kivu Business and higher)

Go to **Staff & roles → Invite staff**, enter the person's name, phone number and role, then send them the invite on WhatsApp. See *Staff & roles* for details.

## 5. Make your first sale

Go to **Point of sale**, tap a product, choose how the customer pays and press **Charge**. See *Selling at the counter*.

## What each page is for

| Page | Use it to |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | See today's sales, profit, expenses, alerts and recent receipts |
| Point of sale | Sell |
| Sales | Find, reprint or void receipts |
| Products | Manage your catalogue and prices |
| Stock movements | Receive deliveries, correct stock and see every unit in and out |
| Suppliers | Record purchases and what you owe |
| Expenses | Record money spent running the business |
| Cash shifts | Count the cash drawer at the start and end of each shift |
| Reports | Profit, best sellers, staff sales, accountant export |
| Staff & roles | Invite staff and control their access |
| Approvals | Decide on voids and stock corrections staff request |
| Plan & billing | Change or renew your plan |
| Settings | Business details, tax and signed-in devices |
