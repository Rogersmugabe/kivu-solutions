# Plan, billing and settings (owners)

## Plans

Prices are in Rwandan Francs.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Kivu Lite | 2,000 / month | Point of sale, products and stock, expenses, reports — one owner |
| Kivu One-Time | 50,000 once | Sales and stock, no staff accounts |
| Kivu Business | 5,000 / month | Adds staff accounts, cash shifts, suppliers and tables |
| Kivu Control | 10,000 / month | Adds approvals for voids, stock changes and free items, and the full audit log |
| Kivu Pro | 20,000 / month | Everything, with priority support |

New shops start with a free trial. **Plan & billing** shows your plan, its status and when the trial or month ends.

### Pay or change plan

1. Pay with **MTN Mobile Money** or **Airtel Money** using the payment details Platinum Tech gives you.
2. Press **Request this plan** (or **Renew**) and confirm.
3. Your plan changes when Platinum Tech confirms the payment. Your current plan stays active until then.

If a subscription expires, a banner appears at the top of every page. Renew to keep using paid features; your data is kept.

Pages your plan does not include show a lock for owners (so you can see what an upgrade adds) and are hidden from staff.

## Settings

- **Business** — business name, type (shop, bar, restaurant…), phone and the **receipt footer** (e.g. *Thank you — no returns after 7 days*). These print on receipts. Choosing Bar or Restaurant turns on Tables & orders.
- **Location** — province, district, sector and cell.
- **Tax** — your TIN and tax rate. Prices include tax; receipts show the tax part.
- **Signed-in devices** — every phone and computer that has used your shop. **Sign out device** for a lost phone or one a former employee used; **Allow again** reverses it.

## Your account

Tap your name at the top right for **Help & user guide** and **Sign out**. On a computer, **Collapse** at the bottom of the menu shrinks it to icons.
