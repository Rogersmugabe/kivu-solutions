# Products and stock movements

## Products

**Products** lists your catalogue with stock levels, cost, selling price and margin. Use the search, category and **Low / Out** filters to find items.

- **Add product** / **Edit** — see *Getting started* for each field.
- **Stock badges** turn amber at the low-stock level and red when you run out. The Dashboard lists them under **Stock alerts**.
- Editing a product never changes its quantity. Quantities change only through sales and stock movements, so every unit is accounted for.
- Stock clerks see quantities and selling prices, but not costs or margins.

## Stock movements

**Stock movements** is the record of every unit in and out of the business:

| Type | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Sold | Every sale line |
| Void returned | Stock put back when a sale is voided |
| Received | Deliveries and supplier purchases |
| Adjustment | Corrections after a count, damage, expiry, theft, own use or returns |
| Wastage | Bar and restaurant losses (spills, breakages, spoiled food) |

Filter by type or product to follow one item. The cards show units in, units out and losses over the last 7 days.

### Receive a delivery

1. **Receive stock**, choose the product (or scan it).
2. Enter the quantity — in base units or bulk units (e.g. *2 crates* = 24 bottles).
3. Owners and managers enter the **total cost paid**. It becomes the product's cost price (*Use current cost* keeps the price you already have). For stock clerks, the current cost is kept automatically.

For deliveries with an invoice or money owed, use **Suppliers → Record purchase** instead — it adds stock and tracks the debt.

### Correct stock (Kivu Control and higher)

1. **Adjust stock** (owners and managers) or **Request adjustment** (stock clerks).
2. Choose *Remove* or *Add*, the quantity and the **reason**.
3. Managers' adjustments apply immediately. Requests wait in **Approvals**; they appear as *Pending* here until approved or rejected.

Kivu will not let stock go below zero.
